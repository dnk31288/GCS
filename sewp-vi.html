Flexible Access to IT Products and Services

NASA Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) VI provides federal agencies a streamlined acquisition pathway for information technology products, services, and solutions.

Through this contract vehicle, government customers can access a broad range of enterprise technology capabilities designed to support modernization, cybersecurity, infrastructure, software, and mission operations.
