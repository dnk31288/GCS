Company Overview

A Trusted Partner for Complex Government Missions

Global Commerce and Services, LLC (GCS) is an SBA-certified HUBZone and Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business providing information technology, professional, and administrative services to government customers.

GCS translates mission and business requirements into practical, executable solutions. Our teams combine technical expertise, disciplined program execution, and hands-on management oversight to deliver reliable results in environments where performance, security, and accountability matter.

Our Mission

Deliver secure, innovative, and cost-effective services that help government customers accomplish critical objectives.

Our Vision

Be a trusted advisor and solutions partner recognized for quality, responsiveness, and measurable mission impact.

Our Approach

Align people, processes, and technology with each customer's priorities, operating environment, and performance expectations.

Leadership

Joaneane A. Smith

Founder, President & Chief Executive Officer

Joaneane A. Smith is the Founder, President, and Chief Executive Officer of Global Commerce and Services, LLC. She brings more than 25 years of experience in management consulting, enterprise information systems, and federal contracting.

Throughout her career, Ms. Smith has led complex technology implementation and business transformation initiatives for organizations including the U.S. Department of the Navy, the U.S. Department of the Army, Smurfit-Stone Container, and the University of Rochester. Her experience spans enterprise systems, program management, technology integration, and organizational modernization.

Under her leadership, GCS has grown into a trusted provider of mission-focused IT and professional services. She remains personally committed to quality, customer success, small-business advancement, and the development of long-term partnerships that deliver measurable value.
